# Email Deliverability Reference

Deliverability is whether your email actually reaches the inbox, not just whether it was sent. Mailbox providers decide where each message goes, the inbox, the spam folder, or nowhere, based on whether you are authenticated, whether you have a good sending reputation, and whether recipients engage with your mail. You earn the inbox by proving who you are, sending to people who want your mail, and keeping them engaged.

This reference covers what makes the difference between sent and delivered.
